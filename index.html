Where this starts

01 · The case

Why this matters now

Community engagement has moved from a nice gesture to something employees, recruits, and clients notice. Treated as a program rather than a series of one offs, the same effort returns culture, visibility, and relationships that last.

See the evidence

Utah ranks first in the country for volunteering, formal and informal alike. The appetite is already inside your workforce. What most companies are missing is not willingness, it is the capacity to organise something at the scale of a whole team.

Source: AmeriCorps and the U.S. Census Bureau, Volunteering and Civic Life in America, 2023.

02 · The strategy

Built around your company

There is no package to pick from. We start with your culture, your calendar, and what your team can realistically carry, then shape the program together, whether that is one very good day or a full year with a theme.

How it works

It begins with a working session with your leadership to decide what your company wants to be known for. From there the calendar gets built, the community partners secured, and the venues, materials, and staffing handled.

You approve the plan. I run everything underneath it.

03 · The return

What it becomes afterward

An event nobody records is a nice afternoon. The numbers, the photography, and the finished story become an impact report your team can actually use: in recruiting, in client proposals, and in the conversation about what next year should look like.

See the deliverables

Participation and output numbers, counted rather than estimated. Event photography. Internal announcement copy in your voice. Social and careers page assets. A one page impact summary your leadership can forward, attach to a proposal, or build next year's program from.

What gets measured is decided at the start, so the numbers you report are the ones you already agreed were worth reporting.
