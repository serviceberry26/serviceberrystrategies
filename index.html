The opportunity

Companies want to be involved in their communities, but the work often happens in pieces. A donation here, a volunteer day there, without a clear plan for how it all fits together.

Serviceberry creates work that matters to the community and brings value back to the company.

Why it matters

Good community engagement can strengthen relationships, give employees something meaningful to be part of, and build a stronger reputation in the community.

The approach

Serviceberry works with your leadership team to understand what matters to your company, where you can make a genuine contribution, and what you want the work to accomplish.

I build the strategy, connect you with the right community partners, and manage the program from planning through execution.

How it works

A year-long program built around your company, with service projects, community partnerships, storytelling, and support along the way.

The impact

Community engagement should create more than a good afternoon. Throughout the year, Serviceberry tracks participation and impact, captures the stories behind the work, and helps your company put them to use.

You get a clear picture of what your investment accomplished and a better way to share it.

What you get

You get a community engagement program built around your company, with meaningful opportunities for your employees to serve, strong community partnerships, and a clear way to measure the impact.

Over time, that work can strengthen your culture, create genuine marketing opportunities, deepen your reputation in the community, and give your team stories worth sharing.

Eliza Rhodes

Founder

My career has taken me through marketing, events, community initiatives, and large-scale service projects. I’ve led marketing teams, produced more than 25 major events, and managed a 400-person service project at the 2026 G20 Interfaith Forum.

My interest in this work started early with Eliza’s Hearts 4 Haiti, a charitable project I have worked on for 20 years that has raised more than $40,000 for healthcare access in Haiti. These experiences have shaped the way I see the role businesses can play in their communities. Companies have people, resources, and reach that can create real impact when they are put to work intentionally.

That’s what led me to Serviceberry. I help companies figure out where they can make a real difference, build the right partnerships, and turn good intentions into work that benefits both the community and the business.
